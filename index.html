Your hair ritual

Four simple steps.

Make oiling a calm and consistent part of your weekly routine.

01

Apply

Take a small amount of oil and apply it gently to your scalp and through your hair.

02

Massage

Massage gently with your fingertips to distribute the oil evenly.

03

Rest

Leave it on for around 30 minutes before washing.

04

Wash

Wash with a gentle shampoo and continue your normal hair-care routine.
